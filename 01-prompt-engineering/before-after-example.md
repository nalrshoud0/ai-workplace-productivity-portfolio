# Before / After Prompt Example

## Before — Weak Prompt
> Write an email about the budget issue.

**Initial Result:** Generic and vague — no audience, no numbers, no reason, no tone specified. AI would likely guess missing details.

## After — C.A.R.E. Structured Prompt
**Context:** I am a Business Analyst on the EAS pilot. The training budget was approved at SAR 15,000 but current estimates show SAR 18,000 is needed due to an added vendor workshop.
**Action:** Write a short email to the project sponsor requesting budget approval for the difference.
**Rules:** State both figures and the reason. Professional, concise tone. No blame, no unconfirmed impacts.
**Expected Output:** Email with subject line + two short paragraphs + closing.

**Improved Result:**
> Subject: EAS Pilot — Training Budget Adjustment Request
> Hello [Sponsor],
> The approved training budget of SAR 15,000 needs to increase to SAR 18,000 due to an added vendor workshop.
> Could you approve the additional SAR 3,000 by Thursday? Happy to walk through the details if needed.
> Best regards, [Name]

## What Improved?
- Audience and purpose are clear
- Exact figures and reason included, nothing invented
- Tone and format defined upfront
- Rules prevented blame or guessed impacts
