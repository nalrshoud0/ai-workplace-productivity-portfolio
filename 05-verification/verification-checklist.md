# Verification Checklist

Before using AI-generated workplace content, I will:
1. Identify content type, audience, and risk level.
2. Compare names, dates, numbers, and decisions against the source.
3. Check that uncertainty and gaps are clearly labeled.
4. Confirm AI didn't invent owners, deadlines, or status.
5. Check tone/format fit the audience.
6. Remove any confidential or sensitive information.
7. Get human sign-off before final use.

## Worked Example
Applied to the first AI draft in Section 3.

| Check | Finding | Action Taken |
|---|---|---|
| Names/dates/numbers | 50 users, Oct 10, Sara matched source | Kept unchanged |
| Owners/deadlines | Config date and training owner were invented | Replaced with [Not Specified] |
| Decisions vs. questions | Second-department expansion wasn't decided | Marked as open decision |
| Sensitive info | All fictional/anonymized | Confirmed safe |

**Result:** Original draft wasn't ready — it invented a deadline and an owner. Corrected before use. Approved as a fictional, trainee-reviewed example.
