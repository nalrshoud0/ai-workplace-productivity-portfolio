# Responsible AI Use

**Accuracy** — I compare AI outputs against the original notes; missing details are labeled, not guessed.


**Accountability** — I remain responsible for the final content; AI only drafts and organizes.


**Confidentiality** — No real EAS project data, credentials, or contracts are used — fictional/anonymized only.


**Privacy** — No real employee or stakeholder names appear in this repository.


**Bias** — I check summaries and priorities for unsupported assumptions before accepting them.


**Human Oversight** — A human makes the final go/no-go and budget decisions; AI never does.

## Green / Amber / Red Classification
| Item | Class | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Public template with fictional examples | Green | Fine for general AI tools |
| Anonymized internal check-in notes | Amber | Use in approved tool only, minimal detail |
| Real employee IDs, credentials, contracts | Red | Never enter into a general AI tool |

**Confirmation:** All examples in this portfolio are fictional. No real sensitive data was used.
