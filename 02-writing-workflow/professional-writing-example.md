# Professional Writing Example
4-step workflow: Draft → Verify → Refine → Human Sign-off

## 1. AI-Generated Draft
> Subject: Urgent Budget Problem
> Hello Team,
> Unfortunately the EAS pilot budget has a shortfall. We now need SAR 18,000 instead of SAR 15,000 because of extra costs. This could delay the whole pilot, so please respond quickly.

## 2. Fact Verification
| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Original budget | Confirmed: SAR 15,000 |
| New estimate | Confirmed: SAR 18,000 |
| Reason | Confirmed: added vendor workshop only |
| "Could delay the whole pilot" | Not confirmed — removed |

## 3. Tone Refinement
Changed "Urgent Budget Problem" to a neutral subject line. Removed "Unfortunately" and the unconfirmed delay claim, which sounded alarmist and unsupported.

## 4. Human Review & Sign-off
Confirmed the final version states both figures, gives the real reason, avoids blame, and drops unverified claims.
**Status:** Approved by trainee (fictional example).

## 5. Final Version
> Subject: EAS Pilot — Training Budget Adjustment Request
> Hello [Sponsor],
> The approved training budget of SAR 15,000 needs to increase to SAR 18,000 due to an added vendor workshop.
> Could you approve the additional SAR 3,000 by Thursday?
> Best regards, [Name]
