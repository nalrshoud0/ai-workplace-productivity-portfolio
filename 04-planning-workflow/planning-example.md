# Planning Workflow Example

**Goal:** Launch a 4-week EAS pilot for one department, with approved requirements, tested approval workflow, trained users, and a go/no-go decision.

**Mechanisms:**
- Requirements control (confirm scope & acceptance criteria)
- Stakeholder updates (short, decision-ready)
- Risk tracking
- Verification of AI-assisted documentation

**Phases & Tasks**
| Phase | Tasks | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Confirm scope | Review notes; list confirmed requirements; hold scope meeting | Approved scope |
| 2. Configure & test | Finish approval-limit config; test workflow; log defects | Tested workflow |
| 3. Train & prepare | Schedule manager training; prepare support FAQ | Ready users |
| 4. Decide | Brief sponsor; document go/no-go decision | Recorded decision |

**Open items:** exact configuration completion date, training owner, and budget increase all need confirmation before Phase 3.
